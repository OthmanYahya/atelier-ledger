# Surface Brief: Atelier Ledger

Scope: Complete local-first personal workstation (Operate mode) uniting a complex to-do & prep engine, private tutoring & lesson tracker across four billing models, and an expense & financial studio.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every task, tutoring session, invoice receivable, and expense shares one crisp architectural ledger instead of splitting across disconnected planners and spreadsheets, refusing marketing hero banners, gradient KPI card stacks, and cluttered kanban chrome.

OWN-WORLD: Warm Architectural Ledger — archival paper canvas (#F6F4EF), crisp white ledger sheets (#FFFFFF) with 1px warm parchment hairlines (#E5E0D5), stone rail (#EFECE4), deep ink (#171614 / #57534E / #8C857B), Deep Botanical Forest primary accent (#1B4D3E), Terracotta highlight (#C2592B), muted Sage/Ochre/Crimson semantic states, optional Ink Dark Mode, Newsreader editorial view titles, Plus Jakarta Sans UI controls, JetBrains Mono timers, and mandatory tabular-nums across all figures.

STORY: Opening directly into Today's operational ledger, the tutor sees overdue/due prep tasks alongside scheduled sessions, launches or logs a timed lesson in one click, inspects any student's exact balance or package credits in a slide-over drawer, and verifies true net effective hourly profit in Finances.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim persistent 232px left navigation rail (Overview, Tasks, Lessons & Calendar, Students & Gigs, Finances & Expenses, Settings, plus active session timer pill) beside an archival-paper Overview workspace anchored by the Newsreader title, immediate Quick-Add / Cmd+K action bar, split two-column operational ledger (Today's Agenda & Scheduled Sessions on the left, Due Prep Tasks & Action Alerts for unpaid receivables and low package credits on the right), and a compact monthly pulse strip.

FORM: User-pinned whole world and first-surface experience (Operate workstation shell with persistent left rail, tabular ledger rows, slide-over edit drawers); position: pinned; seed: none (pinned) "Warm Architectural Ledger" with "slim persistent left navigation rail (Overview, Tasks, Lessons & Calendar, Students & Gigs, Finances & Expenses, Settings)" and "open straight into a functional, high-signal workspace".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
